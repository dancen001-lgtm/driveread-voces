Privacy

This is written to be understood, not to cover our backs.

Last updated: 19 November 2026 · En español

The most important thing first: your books never leave your phone.

The text of your PDFs, the photos you take when scanning and the text recognised from them stay on the device. They aren't uploaded to any server, ours or anyone else's. Character recognition runs inside your phone, with no internet.

And there are no ads and no trackers, on any plan. Your behaviour isn't measured and nothing is sold to anyone.

Without an account

The app works without signing up, and that way almost nothing is collected. The only things that leave your phone are:

  • Your answer to the welcome question — what you'll use the app for — along with a random number for the install that doesn't say who you are.
  • Any suggestion you decide to send, if you send one.

With an account

The account exists so your progress follows you between devices. For that, this is kept on the server:

WhatWhy
Your emailTo identify the account and let you recover it
The book's name, how many pages it has and how far you are So you can pick up on another device where you left off
Your reader name, your portrait and your frame Your profile and the readers' showcase
How long you listened and how many books you finished Your stats and the showcase

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and your name from Google. Nothing else: not your contacts, not your Drive, not your activity.

Not even with an account does your books' content leave the phone. Your clips, the words you correct, the stickers you place and your reminders stay on the device and are not uploaded anywhere. From the book, what travels is the title, how many pages it has, how far you are and whether it's a favourite. Nothing else.

Who else sees anything

WhoWhat they get
Supabase Hosts the database and the accounts. It's where the data above lives
Google Play Services Character recognition and the scanner. They work inside the phone; the images are not sent anywhere
CallMeBot Only if you send a suggestion: the text of that suggestion passes through that service to reach us on WhatsApp. So it's best not to write personal details there

No advertising, no trackers, and no other third party.

When something goes wrong in the app

The app notes down by itself whatever goes wrong while you use it, and every so often sends us a report so it can be fixed. That can't be switched off, so here is exactly what it carries:

  • A random number for the install, which doesn't say who you are.
  • The app version, the Android version and the phone model.
  • What went wrong and how many times.

It carries no name, no email and nothing from your books. It isn't linked to your account. And you can see the same thing that gets sent, inside the app, under Settings → How the app behaved, where you can also erase it.

Deleting everything

From the app: Settings → Your account → Delete my account. It's deleted straight away and can't be undone. The steps and the detail of what gets deleted are on the deletion page.

If you no longer have the app, write to dancen001@gmail.com from the account's email address.

How long it's kept

As long as you have an account. When you delete it everything goes, except from the server's backups, which rotate by themselves within 30 days at most. Suggestions are kept without your name or your email.

Children

DriveRead is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child created an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Security

Everything travels encrypted. Each person can only read their own rows, and that's enforced by the database, not just by the app. Suggestions and the welcome answer can be written but not read: nobody can see what someone else sent.

Changes

If this changes, the date above changes. If the change is a big one, you are told inside the app.
